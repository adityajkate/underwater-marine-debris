# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
General public and academic evaluators. This is a final year university project designed as a public awareness tool and a demonstration of technical capability.

## Product Purpose
To detect and segment submerged marine debris (plastic waste, abandoned fishing gear, metal debris) in underwater survey imagery, providing structured telemetry export. Success means effectively showcasing the AI's detection capabilities for a final year project evaluation.

## Positioning
An academic/final-year project demonstrating production-grade marine debris detection and telemetry mapping.

## Operating Context
Evaluators or public users uploading benthic survey frames, ROV captures, or diver photographic plates directly to the web interface to view bounding boxes and download structured JSON survey reports.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Must not change the existing content/copy currently present on the landing page.
- Single-page application built with React and Vite.
- Processes low-visibility underwater footage.

## Brand Commitments
- **Visual Aesthetic**: Must maintain an ultra-clean, minimalist, brutalist aesthetic (Vercel/Stripe style) featuring stark white backgrounds, crisp borders, and minimalist typography.
- **Imagery**: Must rely exclusively on flat vector imagery. Absolutely no AI-generated glowing orbs, photorealistic photos, or "Studio Ghibli" styles.

## Evidence on Hand
- Existing landing page copy documenting the workflow (Ingestion, Detection, Inspection, Export).
- Pre-existing vector assets for Hero, Workflow, and Research sections.
